## Blocker

### [ ] `ORG-02` — Legal entity has none of the statutory attributes D365 requires — no currency, tax ID, registration number, address, or bank account

**Jenis:** missing-core-capability · **Verifikasi:** CONFIRMED · **Memblokir:** finance-general-ledger, procurement, tax-statutory, cash-bank-management, intercompany-consolidation, management-aset

- **Dynamics 365:** D365 "Create a legal entity" attaches to the legal entity: accounting (functional) currency and reporting currency, ledger and chart of accounts, fiscal calendar, country/region of registration, registration IDs and tax registration (VAT/NPWP-equivalent), primary postal address in the country's address format, bank accounts, and number sequences (reference doc:47-58, 195).
- **Keadaan sekarang:** legal_entities has exactly organization_id, tenant_id, company_code, country_code (char(2)), fiscal_calendar_id, timestamps. country_code is a free-form 2-char string validated only by 'size:2' — there is no countries/regions table to point at and no address format concept. There is no currency column, no currencies table, no tax/registration ID, no address, no bank account.
- **Bukti:** apps/control-plane/database/migrations/2026_07_20_000000_create_core_identity_access_tables.php:43-48; apps/control-plane/database/migrations/2026_07_24_010000_standardize_legal_entity_company_code.php:12-26; apps/control-plane/database/migrations/2026_07_26_010000_create_fiscal_calendar_tables.php:43-45; apps/control-plane/app/Models/LegalEntity.php:16; apps/control-plane/app/Http/Requests/Organization/OrganizationRequest.php:27 (country_code: nullable, size:2, no Rule::exists). docs/dev/01a-tenant-and-org-hierarchy.md:53 promises "legal_entities └── organization_id, tenant_id, company_code, country_code, registration/tax data" — the registration/tax data does not exist. docs/dev/01a:94 explicitly states company_code is NOT a registration number, NPWP, or tax number, yet no field for those was ever added. Grep for currency across docs/dev/*.md returns only docs/dev/15-fiscal-calendars.md:7 mentioning it as a legal-entity consequence in prose.
- **Kenapa penting:** A legal entity with no accounting currency is not an accounting entity. Every money-bearing app is blocked: Procurement cannot state a requisition amount's currency, Management Aset cannot carry an acquisition value or depreciate, and no app can produce a statutory document. Reporting currency absence also blocks group consolidation, which is the main reason a holding buys one tenant with several legal entities (docs/dev/01a:176).
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** Core adds currencies (reference data, tenant-independent) and address_country_regions, then extends legal_entities with accounting_currency_code (NOT NULL for new rows), reporting_currency_code, registration_number, and tax_registration_number, plus a primary-address link into the ORG-01 party layer and a legal_entity_bank_accounts table (or defers bank accounts to a cash-bank-management app while Core owns the party/address). country_code becomes an FK to address_country_regions. Owner: Core — these are legal-entity identity attributes that Core already claims (docs/dev/08:16-35 puts legal_entities in organization_db).

## Tinggi

### [ ] `ORG-01` — No party / global address book model exists anywhere — not in code, not in design

**Jenis:** missing-core-capability · **Verifikasi:** ADJUSTED · **Memblokir:** procurement, global-address-book, tax-statutory, cash-bank-management, human-resources, management-aset

- **Dynamics 365:** D365 F&O Global Address Book: every organization, customer, vendor, worker, contact, and prospect is a party row (DirPartyTable) in ONE shared directory, with DirPartyLocation → LogisticsPostalAddress (country/region, state, county, city, district, street, ZIP, address purposes, effective dates) and LogisticsElectronicAddress (phone, email, URL, telex), plus per-country address formats from LogisticsAddressCountryRegion. Legal entities, operating units, and teams are themselves parties.
- **Keadaan sekarang:** There is no parties table, no addresses table, no contacts table, no phone/email-of-organization anywhere. Organizations have exactly name/classification/status. Legal entities have company_code, country_code, fiscal_calendar_id. Nothing else.
- **Bukti:** Searched every file under apps/control-plane/database/migrations/ for the words party, dirparty, postal_address, address, contact, phone, email_address, bank_account, currency — zero matches. Searched apps/control-plane/app/ for party\|address\|currency\|bank — the only hit is the word "contention" in app/Console/Commands/RunNumberSequenceLoad.php:14. Searched all of docs/ for "address book\|dirparty\|party\|kontak\|contact" — the only hit is the unrelated phrase "Endpoint first-party" at docs/dev/01a-tenant-and-org-hierarchy.md:170. So this is not doc-code drift; the capability has no plan at all.
- **Kenapa penting:** An address book is not a Finance feature, it is the shared substrate for every app. Procurement (app 2) cannot hold a vendor without an address; Management Aset cannot record an asset location or a manufacturer contact; an establishment cannot print its own registration number on an invoice (docs/references/dynamics-365-organization-model.md:80). Without a Core-owned party directory, each app repo will invent its own vendor_addresses / customer_addresses table, and because cross-app DB queries are forbidden the same physical vendor will exist as unrelated rows in every app database — permanently unreconcilable.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** Core owns a new party layer: parties (id, tenant_id, party_type, name), party_postal_addresses (party_id, tenant_id, purpose, country_region_code, state, city, street, postal_code, is_primary, valid_from, valid_to), party_electronic_addresses (party_id, type, value, is_primary), and a link from organizations.id to its party row. Countries/regions and their address formats are Core reference data (address_country_regions, address_formats), the same way fiscal_calendars and number sequences already are. Apps reference party_id as an opaque ID over REST/events and never join to it.
- **Koreksi verifier:** CoreERP has no party, address, contact, or bank-account storage anywhere in code — verified by exhaustive migration grep. What it does have is a target-model diagram (docs/references/drawio/coreerp-saas-organization-hierarchy.drawio:222-223) that puts address, tax registration and bank account directly on legal_entities and operating_units, i.e. an attribute model, NOT D365's shared Global Address Book. So the real gap is not 'no plan' but 'two incompatible plans and neither built': the diagram's attribute model versus the D365 single-directory model the auditor assumes. That decision must be made before procurement starts, because CoreERP forbids cross-app DB queries (AGENTS.md:6) so a shared directory can only ever be a REST contract, never a join. The immediately actionable Core obligation is smaller than the auditor's: a country/region reference table plus a primary postal address and tax/registration fields on legal_entities (which is really ORG-02). A full parties/party_locations layer is a decision to record, not yet a proven requirement.

### [ ] `ORG-03` — Hierarchy restructuring is impossible: version_number is hard-coded to 1 and no second version can ever be created

**Jenis:** missing-core-capability · **Verifikasi:** ADJUSTED · **Memblokir:** procurement, finance-general-ledger, human-resources, intercompany-consolidation

- **Dynamics 365:** D365 organization hierarchy: you copy the published hierarchy into a new draft, edit it, and publish it with an effective date; earlier published versions are retained so historical transactions and reports still resolve against the structure that was effective at their date (reference doc:101, 198; "Operasi yang harus tersedia" #1 at reference doc:272).
- **Keadaan sekarang:** CreateOrganizationHierarchy writes version_number => 1 as a literal and is the only place a version row is ever created. There is no action, route, controller method, or UI affordance to duplicate a published version into a new draft, to reparent a node, or to remove a node. PlaceOrganizationInHierarchy refuses any non-draft version, and once published a version can never return to draft. So a published hierarchy is frozen forever, and the closure/version machinery has never executed with more than one version.
- **Bukti:** apps/control-plane/app/Actions/Organization/CreateOrganizationHierarchy.php:40-45 ('version_number' => 1); grep -rn version_number over app/ database/ tests/ resources/js/ finds only that literal, the column definition (2026_07_20_010000:39,44), an orderByDesc in OrganizationController.php:41, and the UI label at resources/js/pages/settings/organization.tsx:625. Routes are only store / place / publish: apps/control-plane/routes/web.php:108-115. apps/control-plane/app/Actions/Organization/PlaceOrganizationInHierarchy.php:20-22 blocks non-draft. apps/control-plane/app/Actions/Organization/PublishOrganizationHierarchy.php:22-25 is the terminal state. resources/js/pages/settings/organization.tsx:646 renders DraftActions only when version.status === 'draft'.
- **Kenapa penting:** docs/dev/01a-tenant-and-org-hierarchy.md:124 ("Pertahankan version lama untuk transaksi dan laporan historis"), 01a:126 ("Memindahkan organization berarti membuat atau mengubah draft version"), and docs/dev/08-query-scopes-and-schema.md:267 ("Historical version tidak ditulis ulang") describe a capability that cannot be exercised. In practice the first tenant that reorganizes must create a brand-new hierarchy under a different name (the unique(tenant_id, name) constraint forbids reuse) — which orphans every role_assignment_org_scopes.hierarchy_version_id pointing at the old one and silently changes who can see what. docs/dev/08:259 admits "Penggandaan draft dari published version ... masih belum tersedia", so the gap is known but its severity is understated: without it, the whole versioning design is decorative.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** Add a DeriveDraftHierarchyVersion action (copy nodes + closure from the latest published version, version_number = max+1, status draft, caller-supplied effective_from), plus MoveHierarchyNode and RemoveHierarchyNode actions that rebuild the affected closure subtree, and a publish path that marks the previous version superseded/valid_to rather than leaving two rows both 'published'. Needs organization_hierarchy_versions to gain a supersedes/valid_to concept and OrganizationScopeResolver to select by effective date across versions. Owner: Core (Organization).
- **Koreksi verifier:** The capability gap is CONFIRMED and is the largest single hole in this dimension, but the severity is high, not blocker. Nothing here stops a named app from being built or from being built wrong: apps never see hierarchy versions (the only consumers are apps/control-plane/app/Support/CurrentWorkspace.php:64-71 and OrganizationScopeResolver.php:32-44, both internal to Core). What it stops is a real tenant going to production, because the first reorganization requires creating a brand-new hierarchy under a different name (unique(tenant_id, name) at 2026_07_20_010000:27 forbids reuse), which orphans every role_assignment_org_scopes.hierarchy_version_id and silently changes who can see what. Treat as the #1 high item, not a blocker.

### [ ] `ORG-04` — No validate step: publish performs zero structural or purpose validation, and acyclicity holds only by accident

**Jenis:** doc-code-drift · **Verifikasi:** CONFIRMED · **Memblokir:** procurement, tax-statutory, finance-general-ledger

- **Dynamics 365:** D365 validates a draft before publishing: node uniqueness, no cycles, and — critically — that each organization's type is one the hierarchy's purpose accepts (reference doc:88 "purpose ... menentukan jenis organization yang boleh digunakan"; reference doc:272 lists "lakukan validasi" as a required operation between draft and publish).
- **Keadaan sekarang:** Version status is only 'draft' or 'published'; there is no 'validated' state and no validator. PublishOrganizationHierarchy checks the actor's tenant and that status === 'draft', then writes status='published'. Nothing checks that an establishment-purpose hierarchy has operating units under exactly one legal entity, that a legal-reporting hierarchy contains only legal entities, or that the tree is non-trivial. Cycles are structurally impossible today only because the sole mutation appends a fresh leaf under an already-existing node and unique(version_id, organization_id) forbids a second appearance — i.e. the invariant is vacuous, not enforced, and it will break the moment ORG-03's reparent operation is added.
- **Bukti:** apps/control-plane/app/Actions/Organization/PublishOrganizationHierarchy.php:13-26 (no validation beyond status and tenant); grep for 'validated'\|superseded\|expired across app/Actions/Organization/ and app/Models/Organization*.php returns nothing; apps/control-plane/app/Actions/Organization/PlaceOrganizationInHierarchy.php:24-31 (validates tenant + parent existence only, never the hierarchy's purposes); apps/control-plane/database/migrations/2026_07_20_010000_add_organization_structure_and_closure.php:53 (unique(version_id, organization_id)). Claims not met: docs/dev/01a-tenant-and-org-hierarchy.md:122 ("Validasi node unik, tidak bersiklus, dan sesuai jenis organization yang diterima purpose"), 01a:81 ("establishment harus berada di bawah tepat satu legal entity"), 01a:156, docs/dev/08-query-scopes-and-schema.md:141.
- **Kenapa penting:** The establishment rule is the one purpose whose semantics CoreERP has already committed to in code (the 2026_07_22_070000 migration and its test), yet nothing stops an owner from publishing an establishment hierarchy whose root is a cost center, or from placing an establishment under two legal entities across two versions. Any app that later trusts "establishment ⇒ has exactly one owning legal entity" to stamp a registration number on an invoice will emit legally wrong documents.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** Extend hierarchy_purposes with the classifications it accepts and its structural rules (see ORG-05), add a ValidateOrganizationHierarchyVersion action that runs before publish (node uniqueness, acyclicity, classification-per-purpose, establishment-under-exactly-one-legal-entity, root classification), introduce a 'validated' status so publish cannot skip it, and add tests for each rejected shape. Owner: Core (Organization).

### [ ] `ORG-05` — hierarchy_purposes is a bare code/name/description label with none of the semantics D365 attaches to a purpose

**Jenis:** wrong-model · **Verifikasi:** ADJUSTED · **Memblokir:** procurement, finance-general-ledger, tax-statutory

- **Dynamics 365:** In D365 a hierarchy purpose determines four things: which organization types may be used, which application scenarios consume the hierarchy, which parameters/policies are inherited from the parent, and how the hierarchy is used for reporting or business process. Purchasing policies apply only to organizations in a hierarchy of the matching purpose, and when a company has several policy hierarchies a precedence ordering decides which rule wins (reference doc:86-91, 107-111; Microsoft also advises against several hierarchies for the same purpose within one legal entity, reference doc:103).
- **Keadaan sekarang:** hierarchy_purposes is (id, code, name, description, timestamps). It carries no allowed-classification list, no inheritance flag, no precedence, no single-hierarchy-per-purpose rule, and no link to the consuming app. Nothing in the codebase ever reads a purpose to make a decision — CreateOrganizationHierarchy only checks that the codes exist, and OrganizationScopeResolver ignores purpose entirely.
- **Bukti:** apps/control-plane/database/migrations/2026_07_20_010000_add_organization_structure_and_closure.php:13-19; apps/control-plane/app/Models/HierarchyPurpose.php:12 (fillable = code, name, description); apps/control-plane/app/Actions/Organization/CreateOrganizationHierarchy.php:27-30 (existence check only); apps/control-plane/app/Http/Requests/Organization/HierarchyRequest.php:30 (exists:hierarchy_purposes,code). Grep for HierarchyPurpose across app/ finds only the model, the create action, the controller's dropdown feed (OrganizationController.php:53), and the validation rule — no consumer.
- **Kenapa penting:** Purpose is what makes the many-to-many meaningful. Without allowed types, ORG-04's validation has nothing to validate against. Without precedence, the first app that keys a policy to a hierarchy purpose (Procurement, by design — reference doc:249) will have undefined behaviour when a tenant has two policy hierarchies. Nothing yet stops a tenant creating five hierarchies all bearing purpose 'procurement', which Microsoft explicitly warns against.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** Extend hierarchy_purposes with allowed_classifications, allows_parameter_inheritance, allows_multiple_hierarchies, and precedence (or a purpose_rules child table), enforce them in the validate step, and expose purpose semantics on the organization contract so apps can ask "which hierarchy governs procurement policy for this organization". Owner: Core (Organization) for the metadata; consuming apps read it over the contract.
- **Koreksi verifier:** Gap is real and correctly evidenced, but the kind is wrong: this is missing-core-capability, not wrong-model. The existing shape (join table, no purpose_id column) is exactly right and the auditor itself lists it as solid #1 — calling the same table 'wrong-model' contradicts that. The purpose row is simply thin: it lacks allowed_classifications, inheritance, precedence, and a multiple-hierarchies rule. Severity stays high because ORG-04's validator has nothing to validate against until this lands, and because procurement (app 2) is the first designed consumer.

### [ ] `ORG-08` — Cross-tenant and cross-version integrity is enforced only in PHP; the composite foreign keys the design specifies were never created

**Jenis:** contract-gap · **Verifikasi:** CONFIRMED · **Memblokir:** procurement, management-aset, finance-general-ledger

- **Dynamics 365:** D365's company ID is a hard data-security boundary across most of the application (reference doc:49). CoreERP's own design goes further and specifies database-level composite keys.
- **Keadaan sekarang:** organizations has no UNIQUE (tenant_id, id), so no composite FK can point at it. organization_hierarchy_nodes.organization_id, organization_hierarchy_closures.*_organization_id, invitation_codes.organization_id/hierarchy_id, and role_assignment_org_scopes.organization_id/hierarchy_id/hierarchy_version_id are all single-column FKs — the database will happily accept a node whose organization belongs to a different tenant than its hierarchy. organization_hierarchy_nodes.parent_node_id is a plain self-FK, not composite with version_id, so a node can point at a parent in a different version. Only application code prevents both.
- **Bukti:** apps/control-plane/database/migrations/2026_07_20_000000_create_core_identity_access_tables.php:32-41 (organizations: only unique(tenant_id, code), later dropped entirely by 2026_07_24_010000:28-31 — so today organizations has no composite unique at all); apps/control-plane/database/migrations/2026_07_20_010000_add_organization_structure_and_closure.php:47-79. Design specifies otherwise: docs/dev/08-query-scopes-and-schema.md:69 (UNIQUE (tenant_id, id)), :78-79 (FOREIGN KEY (tenant_id, organization_id)), :122-126 (FOREIGN KEY (version_id, parent_node_id)). App-level guards that currently carry the load: apps/control-plane/app/Actions/Organization/PlaceOrganizationInHierarchy.php:17,24-31 and PublishOrganizationHierarchy.php:15. Notably the business app repo already does this correctly: D:/Kerja/app-erp-management-aset/database/migrations/2026_07_26_000000_create_master_data_aset_tables.php:12-15,69-75 adds unique(tenant_id, id) and composite parent FKs with the comment that a cross-tenant parent must be rejected by the database, not by code.
- **Kenapa penting:** The Core holds the tenant boundary for everything else, and its own organization tables are the weakest link — while a downstream app repo is stricter than the Core it depends on. The route for place/publish binds {version} globally (routes/web.php:112-115), so a single missing tenant check in a future writer, an artisan command, a data fix, or an import silently produces a hierarchy that spans tenants, and the closure table will then hand another tenant's organization IDs to CurrentWorkspace::organizations().
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** Add UNIQUE (tenant_id, id) to organizations and organization_hierarchies; carry tenant_id on organization_hierarchy_nodes and organization_hierarchy_closures and make their organization FKs composite on (tenant_id, organization_id); make parent_node_id composite on (version_id, parent_node_id); do the same for invitation_codes and role_assignment_org_scopes. Add a CHECK on organizations.classification while touching the table (see ORG-14). Owner: Core.

### [ ] `ORG-13` — Organization identity is create-only — no rename, no deactivate, no correction, and no company_code locking rule

### [ ] `ORG-17` — No guided setup gate requiring a legal entity — apps are launched with a null legal_entity_id

**Jenis:** doc-code-drift · **Verifikasi:** ADJUSTED · **Memblokir:** management-aset, procurement, finance-general-ledger

- **Dynamics 365:** A legal entity is the company boundary for data security and for any transaction with legal or accounting consequence; D365 has no usable state without one (reference doc:47-58).
- **Keadaan sekarang:** RegisterBusiness creates user, client, tenant, deployment row, owner membership, entitlements, owner role and assignment — and no organization at all. ### [ ] `ORG-M1` — A membership can hold exactly ONE organization scope, and it is copied onto every role — a user cannot be responsible for two unrelated organizations

**Jenis:** wrong-model · **Verifikasi:** VERIFIER-FOUND · **Memblokir:** procurement, management-aset, human-resources, finance-general-ledger

- **Dynamics 365:** docs/references/dynamics-365-organization-model.md:157-163: 'Role assignment juga dapat diberi organization scope: akses hanya untuk organization tertentu atau node beserta turunannya pada hierarchy yang dipilih' — in D365 F&O the organization scope belongs to the individual role assignment, so one user can hold Purchasing Agent scoped to Plant A and Warehouse Clerk scoped to Plant B, and a single assignment can name several organizations.
- **Keadaan sekarang:** UpdateMembership resolves ONE scope from the request and applies that identical scope to every role it creates for the membership. role_assignment_org_scopes carries unique('assignment_id') so an assignment can never have more than one organization. role_assignments carries unique(membership_id, role_id, source) so the same role cannot be assigned twice to the same person with different scopes. ### [ ] `ORG-K1` — Legal entity tidak punya penanda consolidation dan elimination, sehingga invariant "tidak boleh ada jurnal harian" tidak bisa ditegakkan

**Jenis:** missing-core-capability · **Verifikasi:** verifikasi langsung · **Memblokir:** finance-general-ledger, intercompany-consolidation

- **Dynamics 365:** Konsolidasi bukan tipe organisasi baru. Ia dua penanda pada legal entity: *Use for financial consolidation process* dan *Use for financial elimination process*. Aturannya tegas — *"You can't post daily journals in a consolidation company, but you can post them in an elimination company."*
- **Keadaan sekarang:** `legal_entities` hanya punya `organization_id`, `tenant_id`, `company_code`, `country_code`.
- **Bukti:** `apps/control-plane/database/migrations/2026_07_20_000000_create_core_identity_access_tables.php` (definisi `legal_entities`) dan `2026_07_24_010000_standardize_legal_entity_company_code.php`.
- **Kenapa penting:** Ini invariant, bukan fitur pelaporan. Kalau penanda ini ditambahkan setelah ada transaksi, larangan jurnal harian tidak dapat ditegakkan surut — sudah terlanjur ada jurnal di perusahaan yang seharusnya menolaknya. Inilah alasan keputusannya harus diambil di lapisan organisasi, jauh sebelum app Finance ada.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** Tambahkan `is_consolidation_entity` dan `is_elimination_entity` pada `legal_entities`, beserta test yang membuktikan legal entity konsolidasi menolak posting jurnal harian sementara legal entity eliminasi menerimanya. Penegakannya nanti dijalankan app Finance, tetapi penandanya milik Core.
