### [ ] `FIN-20` — Currency, exchange rate types, exchange rates, triangulation, and accounting/reporting/transaction currency are entirely absent

**Jenis:** missing-core-capability · **Verifikasi:** CONFIRMED · **Memblokir:** procurement, management-aset, core-finance-gl, core-tax-id, core-budget, core-cash-bank

- **Dynamics 365:** D365 has Currencies, Exchange rate types, Currency exchange rates (with a triangulation currency for legacy EUR-style pairs), and every ledger declares an accounting currency, a reporting currency, and an exchange rate type. Every amount on every document carries a transaction currency plus its accounting-currency and reporting-currency equivalents. Currency revaluation is a periodic ledger process.
- **Keadaan sekarang:** Nothing. legal_entities has organization_id, tenant_id, company_code, country_code, fiscal_calendar_id, timestamps — no accounting currency, no reporting currency, no exchange rate type. No currency table, no rate table, and no currency column anywhere in the codebase.
- **Bukti:** apps/core/database/migrations/2026_07_20_000000_create_core_identity_access_tables.php:43-48 plus 2026_07_24_010000_standardize_legal_entity_company_code.php:12-14 and 2026_07_26_010000_create_fiscal_calendar_tables.php:43-45 = the complete legal_entities schema; Grep for currency\|exchange_rate across apps/core matches only composer.lock, phpunit.xml, config/database.php:107 (the word 'concurrency'), and two test/command files — zero in migrations, models, or app code. Same grep across D:/Kerja/app-erp-management-aset and D:/Kerja/app-erp-template: no matches.
- **Kenapa penting:** A monetary amount without a currency is not data. Every app that stores money — a purchase order line, an asset acquisition value, a maintenance cost — cannot define its column correctly until this exists. If Procurement ships first with its own currency list, and Asset ships a second one, the two can never be reconciled and cross-app reporting is dead on arrival. This is the single cheapest foundation to build and the most expensive to retrofit.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** CORE. Currency codes, exchange rate types, and exchange rates are shared, tenant-configured, and consumed by every app — exactly the profile of number sequences, which already live in Core. Add currencies, exchange_rate_types, exchange_rates to the control plane; add accounting_currency, reporting_currency, and exchange_rate_type_id to legal_entities (the legal entity is the owner of the currency consequence, per docs/dev/15:7 and the architecture skill). Expose GET /api/internal/v1/currencies and a rate lookup over the internal API. Currency revaluation is a posting process, so it belongs to the Finance/GL app, not Core.

### [ ] `FIN-21` — Unit of measure, UoM classes, and UoM conversions are entirely absent

**Jenis:** missing-core-capability · **Verifikasi:** CONFIRMED · **Memblokir:** procurement, management-aset, core-inventory-costing

- **Dynamics 365:** D365 has Unit of measure with a Unit class (Quantity, Mass, Length, Volume, Time), generic Unit conversions within a class, and product-specific unit conversions on the released product for cases like 1 box = 12 pcs of this item only.
- **Keadaan sekarang:** Nothing. No uom, unit_of_measure, or satuan table, model, or column anywhere in the control plane, the Management Aset app, or the template.
- **Bukti:** Grep for unit_of_measure\|uom\|satuan across apps/core matches only pnpm-lock.yaml and package-lock.json (JS package names). D:/Kerja/app-erp-management-aset/database/migrations contains three migrations (create_management_aset_tables, add_lifecycle_to_m_entitas_aset, create_master_data_aset_tables) with no UoM. No uom in D:/Kerja/app-erp-template.
- **Kenapa penting:** Procurement cannot express a purchase order line — quantity without a unit is meaningless, and purchase UoM to inventory UoM conversion is what makes a receipt post the right quantity. Asset needs it for meter readings, service intervals, and consumable parts on a maintenance checklist. Both apps need the same conversion answer, and neither may read the other's database.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** SPLIT. Core owns the UoM catalog, unit classes, and generic within-class conversions: it is shared reference data, tenant-configured, used by at least two apps, and never app-specific. Product-specific conversions belong to whichever app owns the product master (Procurement/Inventory), because they are a property of the product, which is app business data. Core exposes GET /api/internal/v1/units-of-measure and a conversion endpoint; the app layers its product-specific overrides on top.

### [ ] `FIN-25` — Indonesian statutory localization is entirely absent, and there is nowhere in Core to even store an NPWP

**Jenis:** missing-core-capability · **Verifikasi:** CONFIRMED · **Memblokir:** core-tax-id, procurement, core-finance-gl

- **Dynamics 365:** D365 ships country/region-specific localization per legal entity: registration IDs on the legal entity, tax registration numbers, statutory number sequences, and electronic reporting formats. Indonesia requires NPWP (and NITKU/branch identifier), PKP status, PPN output/input tax, PPh 21/22/23 withholding, e-Faktur/Coretax submission, e-Bupot withholding slips, and a controlled faktur pajak number series.
- **Keadaan sekarang:** legal_entities carries company_code and country_code only. docs/dev/01a-tenant-and-org-hierarchy.md:94 explicitly states company_code is not a registration number, NPWP, or tax number and says such identifiers should be stored as an explicit domain identifier — but no such table or column exists anywhere in Core or in any app.
- **Bukti:** apps/core/database/migrations/2026_07_20_000000_create_core_identity_access_tables.php:43-48 (company_code, country_code) plus 2026_07_24_010000 and 2026_07_26_010000 additions; docs/dev/01a-tenant-and-org-hierarchy.md:94; Grep for npwp\|ppn\|pph\|faktur\|coretax\|bupot across apps/core: no matches
- **Kenapa penting:** The owner is Indonesian and statutory compliance is a real requirement, not optional. Today there is not one column in the system that can hold it, and the documentation forbids reusing company_code for it — correctly, but without providing the alternative. ### [ ] `MISS-FIN-01` — Number issuance has no document date — every fiscal and calendar-year resolution uses the server clock at the moment of the API call

**Jenis:** contract-gap · **Verifikasi:** VERIFIER-FOUND · **Memblokir:** management-aset, procurement, core-finance-gl, core-tax-id

- **Dynamics 365:** In D365 F&O the number sequence scope resolves against the transaction/session date, and a document posted on 2 January with a 31 December document date takes the number and fiscal period belonging to its document date, not to the wall clock. Fiscal-calendar-period scope is resolved from the record's date.
- **Keadaan sekarang:** The internal API accepts only idempotency_key, manual_value, legal_entity_id, and org_unit_id. There is no document_date, transaction_date, or posting_date parameter anywhere in the request, controller, service signature, or contract. Both date-dependent scope values are computed from now(): the calendar-year segment and the calendar-year reset partition, and the fiscal year and period.
- **Bukti:** apps/core/app/Http/Requests/NumberSequence/InternalNumberSequenceRequest.php:17-22 (four rules, no date); apps/core/app/Http/Controllers/NumberSequence/InternalNumberSequenceController.php:35-43 (context() builds only tenant_id, app_id, legal_entity_id, org_unit_id); apps/core/app/Actions/NumberSequence/NumberSequenceService.php:444 ($this->fiscalCalendar->resolve($organization->legalEntity, now())), :452 ('year' => now()->format('Y')), :462-464 (periodKey uses now()->format('Y') for calendar_year); apps/core/app/Actions/FiscalCalendar/FiscalCalendarService.php:117 signature already accepts a date and is simply never given one.
- **Kenapa penting:** This is the single change in this dimension that gets more expensive with every app that integrates, because it is the API SHAPE, not a table. Every backdated document — an asset acquired last month, a purchase invoice received late, a year-end correction — takes the wrong year segment, the wrong fiscal period, and the wrong counter partition. At every 31 December and every fiscal period boundary a batch of documents silently lands in the wrong series, and because period_key partitions the uniqueness indexes the database cannot catch it. The auditor praised the fiscal reset design at length and never noticed that the design cannot express the date it resets on.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** Core: add an optional document_date to the issue and reserve request, thread it through NumberSequenceService::scope() into periodKey(), the year segment, and FiscalCalendarService::resolve() (which already takes a date), and default to today only when the caller omits it. Publish it in the internal OpenAPI before app 2 integrates. Decide and document whether a document_date outside any defined fiscal year is an error (it should be, matching resolve()'s existing fail-rather-than-guess stance).

### [ ] `FIN-02` — No ledger calendar and no period status per module — nothing can open, hold, or permanently close an accounting period

**Jenis:** missing-core-capability · **Verifikasi:** ADJUSTED · **Memblokir:** core-finance-gl, procurement, management-aset, core-tax-id

- **Dynamics 365:** D365 separates the fiscal calendar (shared date definition) from the ledger calendar (LedgerFiscalCalendarPeriod), which carries a per-legal-entity, per-module status of Open / On hold / Permanently closed, plus a per-user-group access list. Every subledger posting validates against it.
- **Keadaan sekarang:** fiscal_periods has only fiscal_year_id, ordinal, name, starts_on, ends_on, timestamps. There is no status column, no module axis, no legal-entity axis on the period, and no ledger table.
- **Bukti:** apps/core/database/migrations/2026_07_26_010000_create_fiscal_calendar_tables.php:31-41; apps/core/app/Models/FiscalPeriod.php:14 (fillable has no status); docs/dev/15-fiscal-calendars.md:52 states 'Belum ada status buka/tutup periode. Menutup periode akuntansi adalah domain ledger, bukan domain kalender.'
- **Kenapa penting:** Period status is the single control that stops a user backdating a document into a closed month. Without it, no app can ever post a financial or legally-consequential document safely, and Indonesian monthly PPN reporting has nothing to lock. The docs defer this to 'domain ledger' — but no ledger exists and no gate in docs/dev/10 tracks it, so it is deferred to nothing.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** The period *definition* stays in Core (it is shared, tenant-configured, cross-app). The period *status* is per legal entity and per module, so it must also live in Core as coordination policy — apps cannot own it because a Procurement invoice and an Asset depreciation run must observe the same closed month, and cross-app DB access is forbidden. Add a ledger_calendar_periods table keyed (legal_entity_id, fiscal_period_id, module) with status open/on_hold/permanently_closed, and expose it read-only over the internal API (see FIN-04).
- **Koreksi verifier:** The absence of period status is real and correctly evidenced. Two adjustments. (a) Severity is high, not blocker: there is no ledger, no journal, and no posting anywhere, so today period status has nothing to protect; it becomes a blocker the moment the first posting app exists. (b) The auditor states as settled that period status 'must also live in Core'. That is a recommendation, not a verified gap — the project's own rule (docs/dev/10-core-foundation-gates.md, decision rule 2: Core holds policy and coordination, business data stays with the owning module) can be read either way, and docs/dev/15:52 currently assigns it to the ledger. The verified gap is that no document, no gate row, and no owner-of-truth exists for period status at all. Present it as an open placement decision to be recorded, not as a Core table to build now.

### [ ] `FIN-04` — No internal API for apps to read a fiscal period or a period status — an app can never validate a posting date

**Jenis:** contract-gap · **Verifikasi:** ADJUSTED · **Memblokir:** management-aset, procurement, core-finance-gl

- **Dynamics 365:** In D365 every module resolves the fiscal period and checks the ledger period status in-process before posting. In CoreERP that resolution lives in Core and apps are forbidden to query Core's database, so it must be an API.
- **Keadaan sekarang:** routes/api.php exposes exactly four internal endpoints, all number-sequence. There is no /api/internal/v1/fiscal-* route and no fiscal shape in the control-plane OpenAPI contract.
- **Bukti:** apps/core/routes/api.php:6-11 (issue, reserve, confirm, cancel only); apps/core/contracts/ contains only apps/procurement.yaml, modules/ (empty), and openapi.json; docs/dev/14-number-sequences.md 'Penerbitan lewat API internal' table lists only the four number-sequence operations.
- **Kenapa penting:** docs/dev/02-module-standard.md:90 requires app data with accounting consequence to carry legal_entity_id, and docs/dev/15 makes the fiscal calendar a legal-entity consequence owned by Core. Together those rules mean an app physically cannot determine which fiscal period its document falls in. Every app will either hard-code a calendar year (silently wrong for a Juli–Juni book year, which docs/dev/15 explicitly calls out as the case to support) or invent its own calendar table, which forks the truth.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** Core: add GET /api/internal/v1/fiscal-position?legal_entity_id&date returning year, period ordinal, period type, and per-module status, behind the same internal-app middleware and throttle. Publish it in the control-plane OpenAPI and emit a fiscal-period-status-changed event on the AsyncAPI channel so apps can invalidate local projections.
- **Koreksi verifier:** No internal API exposes a fiscal period or period status — confirmed. Severity high, not blocker: no app today dates a financial document (Management Aset has no monetary or dated-posting field), so nothing is currently blocked. It becomes mandatory before app 2. Also note this is the narrow case of a wider contract gap I am reporting separately (MISS-FIN-05): Core exposes NO organization or legal-entity read API at all, so an app cannot even list a tenant's legal entities — it receives a single legal_entity_id inside the signed context token (apps/core/app/Support/AppContextToken.php:26) and nothing more. Design the fiscal endpoint as part of a Core reference-data API, not as a one-off.

### [ ] `FIN-07` — reconciliation_pending reservations have no operator remedy, so a dead app outbox permanently gaps a continuous series

**Jenis:** ops-gap · **Verifikasi:** CONFIRMED · **Memblokir:** control-plane, core-tax-id

- **Dynamics 365:** D365's Continuous number sequences rely on the NumberSeqCleanUp batch job, which an administrator runs to recycle numbers left in NumberSequenceList by aborted transactions. It is administrator-driven and always terminating.
- **Keadaan sekarang:** recoverExpired moves expired reservations to reconciliation_pending and deliberately does not release the number. Only confirm() or cancel() clears that state, and both require the reservation's own app_id in context. cancel() is reachable only from the internal app API; there is no admin route, no Inertia page, and no artisan command that can cancel a reservation.
- **Bukti:** apps/core/app/Actions/NumberSequence/NumberSequenceService.php:268-304, :232-266, :797-808 (reservation() filters on app_id); apps/core/routes/api.php:6-11 (cancel is internal-app only); apps/core/routes/web.php:105-107 and :146-149 (number-sequence web/api routes are index, update, advance only); apps/core/app/Console/Commands/RecoverNumberSequenceReservations.php:38-45 (logs the backlog but cannot drain it)
- **Kenapa penting:** availableContinuousNumber selects only status='available', so a pending row is skipped and higher numbers are issued around it. A continuous sequence is therefore gap-free only if every reservation is eventually resolved by the owning app. If an app is uninstalled, its outbox is lost, or its database is restored from a backup taken before the reserve, that number is a permanent hole with no human able to close it. For an Indonesian faktur pajak series, an unaccounted-for number is a compliance problem, not a cosmetic one.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** Core: add an admin-facing reconciliation queue on the Nomor dokumen page listing pending reservations with age and owning app, plus an authorised force-cancel path (a manage-number-sequences gated route, and an artisan command) that writes an audit event with an operator user_id and a reason. Keep the app-driven path as the normal case; this is the break-glass.

### [ ] `FIN-16` — There is no way to return a number after it has been issued or confirmed — no void, no cleanup, no release endpoint

**Jenis:** missing-core-capability · **Verifikasi:** CONFIRMED · **Memblokir:** core-tax-id, procurement, core-finance-gl

- **Dynamics 365:** D365 recycles numbers from deleted or aborted documents for continuous sequences via the cleanup job, and statutory continuous series require that every allocated number be either used or explicitly voided and reported.
- **Keadaan sekarang:** cancel() only accepts a reservation in status reserved or reconciliation_pending; a confirmed reservation is explicitly refused. There is no endpoint or method that voids a number_sequence_issues row or marks a confirmed pool number as voided. number_sequence_reusable_numbers is written only by cancel() and read only by nextDirectNumber, so it is unreachable for the non-continuous preallocated path — a non-continuous number can never be given back at all.
- **Bukti:** apps/core/app/Actions/NumberSequence/NumberSequenceService.php:239-241 (confirmed cannot be cancelled), :254-261 (reusable written only on cancel), :587-599 (reusable read only in nextDirectNumber, which runs only when preallocation_enabled is false); apps/core/routes/api.php:6-11 (no void endpoint)
- **Kenapa penting:** Business documents get voided. In D365 that is a first-class flow. Here, once an app confirms a number and then the user cancels the document, Core believes the number is live forever, and there is no record distinguishing 'used' from 'voided'. Indonesian faktur pajak reporting requires cancelled invoice numbers to be declared as cancelled, which needs a voided state Core does not have.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** Core: add POST /api/internal/v1/number-sequence-issues/{id}/void, an is_voided/voided_at column plus a void reason on number_sequence_issues, and for continuous sequences a decision recorded in docs: either the voided number returns to the pool (true D365 cleanup) or it stays consumed and is reported as cancelled. ### [ ] `MISS-FIN-02` — The application runs in UTC and nothing in Core carries a tenant or legal-entity time zone, so date-partitioned numbering is wrong for seven hours a day in Indonesia

**Jenis:** missing-core-capability · **Verifikasi:** VERIFIER-FOUND · **Memblokir:** control-plane, management-aset, procurement, core-tax-id

- **Dynamics 365:** D365 F&O stores UTC and resolves every date-sensitive posting through the legal entity's time zone (and the user's preference), which is why a company in WIB never sees a document dated to the previous day.
- **Keadaan sekarang:** config/app.php sets timezone UTC. Grep for timezone\|time_zone across every migration and model in the control plane returns zero hits — tenants, organizations, and legal_entities all carry no time zone. now() therefore evaluates in UTC everywhere it partitions or renders a date.
- **Bukti:** apps/core/config/app.php:68 ('timezone' => 'UTC'); grep -rn 'timezone\|time_zone\|locale' over apps/core/database/migrations and apps/core/app/Models returns nothing; consumers of the UTC clock: apps/core/app/Actions/NumberSequence/NumberSequenceService.php:444, :452, :462-464.
- **Kenapa penting:** Jakarta is UTC+7. Every document numbered between 00:00 and 07:00 local time resolves against the PREVIOUS UTC day. On 1 January that means the previous calendar year segment and the previous year's counter partition; on the first of a fiscal period it means the previous period. Combined with MISS-FIN-01 this makes the reset-period feature — the part of this dimension the auditor rated most highly — unreliable at exactly the boundaries where a statutory series must be exact. It also silently mis-dates issued_at on every audit record used as the compliance trail.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** Core: add a time zone to the legal entity (it is a legal-entity consequence in the same family as fiscal calendar and currency, per docs/dev/15-fiscal-calendars.md), resolve every date-partitioning and date-rendering call through it rather than through the raw UTC clock, and keep storage in UTC. Do this in the same change as MISS-FIN-01 — a document date without a time zone to interpret it in is still ambiguous.

### [ ] `MISS-FIN-05` — Core exposes no read API for legal entities or organizations at all, so an app cannot list the entities its own financial documents must be stamped with

**Jenis:** contract-gap · **Verifikasi:** VERIFIER-FOUND · **Memblokir:** management-aset, procurement, core-finance-gl, core-tax-id

- **Dynamics 365:** In D365 every module reads the legal entity table in-process.
