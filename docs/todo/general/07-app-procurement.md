### [ ] `PROC-01` — Procurement does not exist as a release unit: no repo, no manifest, no catalog row, no permissions, no number sequences

**Jenis:** missing-app · **Verifikasi:** CONFIRMED · **Memblokir:** procurement-core, procurement-sourcing, ap-invoicing, asset-management spare-part and service purchase, asset acquisition from receipt

- **Dynamics 365:** D365 F&O Procurement and sourcing is a full module: purchase requisitions, RFQ, purchase agreements, purchase orders with change management and versioning, product receipts with over/under tolerance, vendor invoices with 2-way/3-way matching, vendor collaboration, consignment, purchasing policies scoped by organization hierarchy purpose, procurement category hierarchy and category policies, and vendor evaluation.
- **Keadaan sekarang:** Nothing. There is no `D:/Kerja/app-erp-procurement` repo (only `app-erp-template` and `app-erp-management-aset` exist beside CoreERP). Inside CoreERP the `apps` row for `procurement` was deleted, taking its `app_entry_points`, `permissions`, `security_privileges`, `security_duties` and (later-added) `app_number_sequence_references` with it via cascade, and its entitlements and placements by explicit delete. The only two live references to the id `procurement` in code are test fixtures.
- **Bukti:** apps/core/database/migrations/2026_07_23_020000_remove_procurement_development_app.php:10-15 and :18-21 (down() intentionally does not restore); cascade paths at apps/core/database/migrations/2026_07_20_000000_create_core_identity_access_tables.php:130,141,150,166; surviving fixture-only mentions at apps/core/tests/Feature/Auth/RegistrationTest.php:36 and apps/core/tests/Feature/ControlPlane/ProviderIdentityMonitorTest.php:30,47; searched the whole tree for a procurement migration/model/controller/app.yaml — none exists.
- **Kenapa penting:** Procurement is the owner's declared app #2 and the reference baseline is D365 F&O, where Procurement is one of the largest modules and the single biggest consumer of the organization model, workflow, number sequences and financial dimensions. Starting it as one app is also the wrong unit: with per-app databases and no cross-app joins, a single `procurement` app would have to own vendor master, product master, inventory receipt and AP invoice, which are separately-released capabilities in D365 and belong to different owners.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** Owner: Provider/platform decision, then per-app teams. (1) Do not create one `procurement` app. Split into the release units listed in `apps_implied`. (2) The buildable-today slice is `procurement-core`: procurement category hierarchy + category policy, buyer groups, terms of payment, delivery terms, mode of delivery, misc charges codes, requisition purpose codes, and a purchase requisition header/line with `tenant_id` + `legal_entity_id` + `org_unit_id`, requisition number from Core, vendor and item held as opaque external references with a display snapshot, status stopping at `submitted`. (3) Everything past `submitted` — approval, budget check, PO confirmation, receipt, invoice — is gated on CORE-01, CORE-02, CORE-07, MD-01..MD-05 below and must not be faked with a local status column. (4) Scaffold from `D:/Kerja/app-erp-template`, register via `POST /api/v1/provider/apps` per docs/dev/13-publishing-an-app-release.md:37-39, and set `contract_url` to the app repo's published OpenAPI rather than adding a file under `apps/core/contracts/apps/`.

### [ ] `CORE-01` — No event backbone exists: no outbox, no inbox, no broker, no AsyncAPI channel anywhere in Core or in the one shipped app

**Jenis:** missing-core-capability · **Verifikasi:** CONFIRMED · **Memblokir:** procurement-core, inventory, finance-gl, ap-invoicing, budget, asset-management integration, reporting projections

- **Dynamics 365:** D365 F&O runs Procurement, Inventory and Finance in one database with in-process posting, so it needs no broker. CoreERP has explicitly chosen per-app databases with no cross-database joins, which makes the event backbone the load-bearing replacement for that in-process posting — it is not optional.
- **Keadaan sekarang:** Grepping `apps/` for `outbox`, `inbox`, `asyncapi`, `kafka` or `rabbit` matches exactly one file, and that is a number-sequence test. Core publishes no domain events. `deploy/` contains only `compose/README.md` and `web-shell.nginx.conf.example` — no broker service. The one shipped business app declares `channels: {}`.
- **Bukti:** grep for `outbox\|Outbox\|asyncapi\|AsyncAPI\|kafka\|rabbit` over apps/ returned only apps/core/tests/Feature/ControlPlane/NumberSequenceTest.php; D:/Kerja/app-erp-management-aset/contracts/asyncapi.yaml (3 lines, `channels: {}`); D:/Kerja/app-erp-template/contracts/asyncapi.yaml (same); `find deploy -type f` returns 2 files; the mandate is docs/dev/04-api-and-integration.md:14-30 (broker as backbone), :34-51 (event naming, required metadata, outbox-in-same-transaction, inbox dedup) and docs/dev/12-external-module-integration.md:69.
- **Kenapa penting:** Every meaningful Procurement outcome is a cross-database fact: a product receipt increases inventory on-hand, creates a GL accrual, and may create a fixed asset in Management Aset; a posted vendor invoice reverses the accrual and creates an AP liability; a cancelled PO releases a budget reservation. With no outbox there is no way to make the app-local transaction and the cross-app fact atomic, and with no inbox a retry produces a duplicate receipt or a duplicate accrual. This single gap is what makes Procurement unbuildable more than any missing master data.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** Owner: Core defines the contract and operates the broker; each app owns its own outbox/inbox tables. (1) Choose and deploy a broker in `deploy/` (Redis Streams is sufficient for v1; document the ADR). (2) Publish a Core-owned envelope package or contract carrying `id`, `type`, `occurred_at`, `tenant_id`, `legal_entity_id`, `org_unit_id`, `correlation_id`, `data` exactly as docs/dev/04-api-and-integration.md:36-47 specifies. (3) Add `outbox_events` and `inbox_events` to `D:/Kerja/app-erp-template/database/migrations/` plus a publisher worker and an idempotent consumer base class, so the scaffold ships the pattern rather than each app inventing it. (4) Extend the catalog registration payload to carry the app's AsyncAPI url alongside `contract_url` and validate that every published channel name matches `<app>.<aggregate>.<action>.vN`.

### [ ] `CORE-02` — There is no authenticated app-to-app call path and no service discovery — Procurement cannot call Inventory, and AP cannot call Procurement

**Jenis:** missing-core-capability · **Verifikasi:** CONFIRMED · **Memblokir:** ap-invoicing three-way match, procurement-core receipt to inventory, asset-management requisition for work order, budget check

- **Dynamics 365:** Not applicable in-process in D365; in CoreERP's chosen architecture this is the REST half of the integration contract that docs/dev/04-api-and-integration.md:5-12 mandates for commands and data reads.
- **Keadaan sekarang:** `app_service_credentials` and the `internal-app` middleware authenticate an app calling Core, and the only routes behind them are the four number-sequence endpoints. There is no registry of app base URLs per placement, no Core-issued token an app can present to another app, no manifest field declaring which other apps this app consumes, and no provider approval step for such a grant. `deploy/compose.fragment.yaml` in the one shipped app hardcodes `COREERP_URL: http://core-app` and nothing else.
- **Bukti:** apps/core/routes/api.php:6-11 (entire internal API surface); apps/core/bootstrap/app.php:20; apps/core/database/migrations/2026_07_24_030000_create_number_sequence_tables.php:146-160; D:/Kerja/app-erp-management-aset/deploy/compose.fragment.yaml:1-14; `app_dependencies` sekarang hanya menyatakan prerequisite lifecycle, bukan app yang dikonsumsi atau scope panggilannya; requirement contract tetap ada di docs/dev/04-api-and-integration.md:5-12.
- **Kenapa penting:** Three-way invoice matching is a synchronous read: AP must fetch the PO line and the receipt line quantities and prices at the moment of matching, not from an eventual projection, because tolerance evaluation must be deterministic and auditable. Same for a requisition raised by Management Aset for a work order — it needs a synchronous `POST /api/v1/purchase-requisitions` with an idempotency key and a source reference. Events alone cannot carry these; docs/dev/04-api-and-integration.md:12 says so explicitly.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** Owner: Core. (1) Add an app service registry keyed by (app_id, placement) holding the internal base URL, populated by the deployment worker when a placement reaches `ready`. (2) Add a `consumes:` block to `app.yaml` listing the app ids and permission scopes this app calls, validated at catalog registration and requiring provider approval; reject cycles in that separate consumption graph. `dependsOn` hanya mengatur prerequisite lifecycle. (3) Issue short-lived caller tokens from Core (`POST /api/internal/v1/app-tokens` with audience = callee app) and have callee apps verify audience + tenant + granted scope. (4) Reuse the `Idempotency-Key` + `(connection_id, document_id, line_id)` uniqueness rule already written down at docs/dev/12-external-module-integration.md:67 for app-to-app commands too.

### [ ] `CORE-04` — The app context token carries one legal entity and one operating unit, not the user's authorized organization scope — apps cannot filter records by scope

**Jenis:** wrong-model · **Verifikasi:** CONFIRMED · **Memblokir:** procurement-core, ap-invoicing, inventory, every future app with organization-scoped records

- **Dynamics 365:** D365 evaluates organization scope per role assignment and applies it as a record-level filter on every purchase requisition, PO and invoice query. A buyer scoped to a division sees that division's subtree, not one hand-picked unit.
- **Keadaan sekarang:** The token payload is `{iss, aud, sub, tenant_id, legal_entity_id, org_unit_id, permissions[], iat, exp}` — a single selected pair from the session workspace, plus a flat permission list. There is no set of authorized organizations, no `include_descendants`, no hierarchy or purpose, and no way for the app to ask. The one shipped app reads `legal_entity_id`/`org_unit_id` into request attributes and then never uses them for anything.
- **Bukti:** apps/core/app/Support/AppContextToken.php:24-34; apps/core/routes/web.php:92-98 (issued from `CurrentWorkspace::legalEntity/operatingUnit`); apps/core/app/Support/CurrentWorkspace.php:88-101 (single selection per classification); D:/Kerja/app-erp-management-aset/api/app/Http/Middleware/RequireCoreErpContext.php:20-21 and a repo-wide grep showing `legal_entity` appears exactly once in that app's PHP — it is read and discarded; the required query shape is specified at docs/dev/08-query-scopes-and-schema.md:210-224 and the authorization order at :248-256.
- **Kenapa penting:** docs/dev/08-query-scopes-and-schema.md:253-255 requires step 4 (resolve organization scope and effective hierarchy version) before step 5 (apply predicates). Core performs step 4 for its own screens but does not transmit the result. So a Procurement app can only ever show 'the one org I picked in the header' or 'everything in the tenant' — the first is unusable for a buyer covering a division, the second is a data-leak. This is a modelling error, not a missing feature: the token shape encodes a UI selection where the domain needs an authorization result.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** Owner: Core. Extend the context token (or add `GET /api/internal/v1/context/organization-scope`) to return the resolved scope: the active legal entity, the active operating unit, and the list of authorized organization ids per permission — expanded through the closure of the effective hierarchy version, with the hierarchy id, version id and purpose named so the app can pin historical scope. Keep the token small by returning scope ids for the app's own permissions only. Update `D:/Kerja/app-erp-template` to carry `legal_entity_id`/`org_unit_id` columns and a scope-applying query trait so no app has to reinvent it.

### [ ] `PROC-MG-01` — Core exposes no internal API for organization or legal-entity identity, so an app can stamp legal_entity_id / org_unit_id but can never render or print them

**Jenis:** missing-core-capability · **Verifikasi:** VERIFIER-FOUND · **Memblokir:** procurement-core requisition list and PO document, ap-invoicing, every app that stamps an organization id

- **Dynamics 365:** In D365 a purchase requisition shows the requesting operating unit's name and a purchase order prints the buying legal entity's name, address and registration numbers, because Procurement and the organization model share one database. CoreERP's per-app-database rule removes that, and REST is the mandated replacement (docs/dev/04-api-and-integration.md:5-12).
- **Keadaan sekarang:** The entire internal API is four number-sequence endpoints (apps/core/routes/api.php:6-11). There is no endpoint to read an organization, a legal entity, or the tenant's organization list. The context token carries only opaque ULIDs for legal_entity_id and org_unit_id. The app launch manifest returns the tenant name and app list only. Worse, migration 2026_07_24_010000_standardize_legal_entity_company_code.php:28-31 drops organizations.code entirely, so operating units now have no human-readable code anywhere in Core — docs/dev/01a-tenant-and-org-hierarchy.md:98 confirms this is intentional for operating units.
- **Bukti:** apps/core/routes/api.php:1-11 (complete internal surface); apps/core/app/Support/AppContextToken.php:24-34; apps/core/app/Http/Controllers/AppLaunchManifestController.php:17-25; apps/core/database/migrations/2026_07_24_010000_standardize_legal_entity_company_code.php:28-31; D:/Kerja/app-erp-management-aset/api/app/Http/Middleware/RequireCoreErpContext.php:19-22 (attributes set, never resolved to anything displayable).
- **Kenapa penting:** This breaks the very first screen of the minimum-viable slice the auditor himself proposes. A requisition list showing 'Unit: 01JQ8Z...' is unusable and violates the everyday-Indonesian rule. It also blocks the PO document outright, since the buying legal entity's display name and company_code cannot be obtained. The auditor buried this inside CORE-10's required_change ('expose all of it read-only over GET /api/internal/v1/legal-entities/{id}') and never raised it as a finding, so it would not appear on a roadmap.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** Owner: Core. Add read-only internal endpoints behind the existing internal-app middleware: GET /api/internal/v1/legal-entities/{id} and GET /api/internal/v1/organizations/{id} returning id, name, classification, status, and for legal entities company_code and country_code; plus a bulk/list form so a 200-row grid does not fan out. Server derives tenant from the credential, never from the request body. This is small, unblocks the first screen, and is a prerequisite for the snapshot-versus-live-lookup rule in CORE-21 (the name is a snapshot; the id is the reference).

### [ ] `PROC-04` — The `procurement` hierarchy purpose is seeded but has no consumer, no precedence, and no resolver — the one thing D365 uses it for

**Jenis:** missing-core-capability · **Verifikasi:** CONFIRMED · **Memblokir:** procurement-core purchasing policies, requisition control, category access, catalog policy, budget scoping

- **Dynamics 365:** D365 purchasing policies apply only to organizations in a hierarchy whose purpose matches the policy, and when an organization sits in more than one such hierarchy, hierarchy precedence decides which policy wins. Policy rule types are: requisition purpose, category access, catalog policy, requisition control, reallocation, and replenishment category access.
- **Keadaan sekarang:** `hierarchy_purposes` contains a row `procurement` seeded at migration time, and `organization_hierarchy_purposes` allows a hierarchy to carry several purposes. Nothing anywhere resolves "the published, effective hierarchy version carrying purpose P for tenant T". There is no precedence column or table. `role_assignment_org_scopes` stores `hierarchy_id`/`hierarchy_version_id` but never a purpose, and `OrganizationScopeResolver` picks whatever hierarchy the caller names without checking its purpose.
- **Bukti:** apps/core/database/migrations/2026_07_20_010000_add_organization_structure_and_closure.php:89 (seed), :29-33 (link table, no precedence column), :67-79 (role_assignment_org_scopes, no purpose column); apps/core/app/Support/OrganizationScopeResolver.php:33-49 (resolves by hierarchy id only); grep for `precedence` across apps/core/app returned nothing; the design intent is stated at docs/references/dynamics-365-organization-model.md:109-111 and docs/dev/01a-tenant-and-org-hierarchy.md:149.
- **Kenapa penting:** This is precisely the capability the D365 research was done for, and Procurement is its first and main consumer. Without it a purchasing policy cannot be scoped, cannot be resolved deterministically when two hierarchies apply, and category access / requisition control / catalog policy cannot be implemented at all — which means requisitions cannot be constrained, which means approval and budget check have nothing well-defined to act on.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** Owner: Core. (1) Add `organization_hierarchy_purposes.precedence` (integer, unique per purpose per tenant) and enforce it. (2) Add an internal API `GET /api/internal/v1/organization-hierarchies/effective?purpose=procurement&as_of=<ts>` returning hierarchy id, version id, effective_from and the ordered precedence list — the server chooses purpose and version, the caller never sends parent/depth/hierarchy_id, per docs/dev/12-external-module-integration.md:25. (3) Add `purpose_code` to `role_assignment_org_scopes` so an access scope and a policy scope are distinguishable. (4) Publish `organization.hierarchy-version.published.v1` so apps can maintain the local projection that docs/dev/08-query-scopes-and-schema.md:169-185 already specifies as `procurement_org_hierarchy_projection`.

### [ ] `CORE-03` — One symmetric context-signing key is shared by Core and every app, so any app can forge another app's context token

**Jenis:** contract-gap · **Verifikasi:** ADJUSTED · **Memblokir:** ap-invoicing, party-master vendor bank details, segregation of duties

- **Dynamics 365:** D365 uses AAD-issued tokens with per-service audiences and asymmetric signing; no service holds the key that mints another service's tokens.
- **Keadaan sekarang:** `config/coreerp.php` reads a single `COREERP_APP_CONTEXT_SIGNING_KEY`. Core signs HS256 context tokens with it, and the same value is injected into every app container. The app-side verifier checks `aud` against its own app id — but it holds the key that can produce any `aud`. The token also has no `jti`, so it is replayable for its full five-minute life.
- **Bukti:** apps/core/config/coreerp.php:16; apps/core/app/Support/AppContextToken.php:18-35 (HS256, no jti, 300s exp); D:/Kerja/app-erp-management-aset/api/app/Http/Middleware/RequireCoreErpContext.php:36-42 (verifies with the same shared secret); D:/Kerja/app-erp-management-aset/deploy/compose.fragment.yaml:9 (`COREERP_APP_CONTEXT_SIGNING_KEY: ${COREERP_APP_CONTEXT_SIGNING_KEY}` — same variable for every service); the design is described at docs/dev/13-publishing-an-app-release.md:80.
- **Kenapa penting:** Today there is one business app, so the blast radius is nil. The moment Procurement, AP, Inventory and a vendor master coexist, a compromise or a bug in the least sensitive app yields tokens that read vendor bank accounts and post vendor invoices in the most sensitive one. D365's canonical SoD example — the person who creates a vendor must not be the person who pays the vendor — becomes unenforceable if any app can mint any identity.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** Owner: Core. Move to asymmetric signing: Core holds an Ed25519/RS256 private key and publishes a JWKS endpoint; apps verify with the public key and hold no signing material. Add `jti` and have apps reject replays within the token lifetime. If symmetric signing must be kept short-term, derive a per-app key (HKDF over app_id) so no app holds another app's key, and rotate on release.
- **Koreksi verifier:** The technical facts are all correct — one symmetric key, no jti, every app holds the key that can mint any audience — but this is a documented deliberate design (docs/dev/13:80), not drift, and it does not stop Procurement being built. The fix is contained inside two verify/issue functions plus a JWKS endpoint, so it is rework-before-production, not a build blocker. Severity high, kind design-weakness rather than contract-gap. It does become severe the moment a vendor master and an AP app coexist with Procurement.

### [ ] `CORE-05` — Legal entity and operating unit are chosen independently and never validated as related, so a document can be stamped with an incoherent pair

**Jenis:** missing-core-capability · **Verifikasi:** CONFIRMED · **Memblokir:** procurement-core, ap-invoicing, finance-gl, any legal-entity-scoped document

- **Dynamics 365:** In D365 an operating unit's relationship to a legal entity is established by its placement in an effective hierarchy; a purchase requisition's requesting operating unit and its legal entity are always consistent because the LE is derived, not picked.
- **Keadaan sekarang:** `WorkspaceContextController::update` resolves the legal entity and the operating unit as two independent lookups against the same flat list of organizations the user may see. Nothing checks that the chosen operating unit sits under the chosen legal entity in any hierarchy version. `CurrentWorkspace::selected()` does the same for the session default. The pair then goes straight into the context token and, per docs, into every app transaction row.
- **Bukti:** apps/core/app/Http/Controllers/Organization/WorkspaceContextController.php:24-27 and :41-50 (`selection()` filters by classification only); apps/core/app/Support/CurrentWorkspace.php:94-101; consumed at apps/core/routes/web.php:96-97; the rule it breaks is docs/dev/01a-tenant-and-org-hierarchy.md:130-136 and :81 (an establishment must sit under exactly one legal entity in the effective version).
- **Kenapa penting:** A purchase requisition or vendor invoice carrying `legal_entity_id = PT-A` and `org_unit_id = a department of PT-B` is an accounting error that no downstream app can detect, because no downstream app is allowed to query Core's hierarchy tables. It will surface as unexplainable intercompany balances long after the documents are posted, and it is silently reachable today by any user with access to two legal entities.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** Owner: Core. On workspace activation, validate that the selected operating unit is a descendant of the selected legal entity in the effective version of a hierarchy carrying a purpose that admits that pairing (default: the establishment purpose; procurement policies may name the procurement purpose). Reject incoherent pairs with everyday Indonesian wording, e.g. "Unit kerja ini tidak berada di bawah perusahaan yang dipilih". Where only one legal entity is possible for the chosen unit, derive it rather than asking.

### [ ] `CORE-06` — No hierarchy projection contract to app databases — the pattern is fully specified in docs and entirely unimplemented

**Jenis:** doc-code-drift · **Verifikasi:** ADJUSTED · **Memblokir:** procurement-core scoped lists, purchasing policy resolution, reporting

- **Dynamics 365:** D365 resolves organization hierarchy in-process. CoreERP's substitute is a per-app local projection of the closure, which docs/dev/08-query-scopes-and-schema.md already names for Procurement specifically.
- **Keadaan sekarang:** docs/dev/08-query-scopes-and-schema.md gives the exact table (`procurement_org_hierarchy_projection`), the exact descendants query joining it, and the rule that publishing a new version must not delete projections older versions still need. None of it exists: Core publishes no hierarchy event (CORE-01), the template ships no projection table, and docs/dev/10-core-foundation-gates.md:19 already lists this as `Belum` with the gate "a real module needs descendants query" — Procurement is exactly that module.
- **Bukti:** docs/dev/08-query-scopes-and-schema.md:43-47, :169-185, :210-224; docs/dev/10-core-foundation-gates.md:19; docs/dev/08-query-scopes-and-schema.md:257-259 admits projection is not available; no `projection` table in D:/Kerja/app-erp-template/database/migrations/ (only .gitkeep) or in D:/Kerja/app-erp-management-aset/database/migrations/.

Without a local projection the app must either call Core per row (forbidden and slow) or query Core's database (architecturally forbidden). Enforce the retention rule from docs/dev/08-query-scopes-and-schema.md:185 — old versions stay until no transaction, authorization result or report references them. - **Koreksi verifier:** The gap is real and precisely as described. But it is not independently blocking: it is the consumer half of CORE-01 (there is no event to project from) and cannot be started before the broker exists. The app's own requisition schema (tenant_id + legal_entity_id + org_unit_id) is unchanged by it, so Procurement can start and add the projection later without rework. Severity high, not blocker. The auditor's claim that 'the gate condition is now met' is premature — Management Aset is master-data CRUD with no descendants query in code; Procurement would be the first real consumer, and it does not exist yet.

### [ ] `CORE-07` — No workflow / approval engine, and it is not even in the foundation gates matrix although the project's own design diagram names it

**Jenis:** missing-core-capability · **Verifikasi:** ADJUSTED · **Memblokir:** procurement-core requisition approval, PO change management, ap-invoicing invoice approval, party-master prospective vendor approval, asset-management disposal approval

- **Bukti:** docs/references/drawio/organization-hierarchy-concept-and-impact.drawio:118,121-131 (shared approval executor, per-module rules, "status purchase request berubah hanya setelah hasil approval diterima") and :97 (extract to core only after two modules need it); docs/dev/05-customization-and-addons.md:10 ("purchase > 100 juta perlu 2 approval") and :20 (rules as validated DSL with version, audit, rollback); docs/dev/10-core-foundation-gates.md:7-20 — no workflow row; grep for `workflow\|approval` over apps/core/app returned no domain code.

### [ ] `PROC-STAT-01` — Indonesian statutory procurement requirements have no design landing point: faktur pajak masukan, NPWP/PKP validation, PPN input, PPh withholding and e-Bupot

**Jenis:** missing-core-capability · **Verifikasi:** ADJUSTED · **Memblokir:** ap-invoicing, party-master, tax-id, procurement-core PO tax computation

- **Dynamics 365:** D365 handles these through Tax (sales tax groups, item sales tax groups, withholding tax groups and codes on the vendor), Electronic invoicing, and country-specific localizations. Indonesia is not a Microsoft-localized country in the base product, so this is bespoke either way.
- **Keadaan sekarang:** Nothing. Core has no tax registration field on the legal entity (CORE-10), no currency (CORE-11), no tax period (CORE-14), no vendor master to hang an NPWP or a withholding group on, and no tax app. The only acknowledgement in the repo is docs/dev/01a-tenant-and-org-hierarchy.md:94 stating that `company_code` is not an NPWP — a correct disclaimer with nothing on the other side of it.
- **Bukti:** docs/dev/01a-tenant-and-org-hierarchy.md:94; apps/core/database/migrations/2026_07_20_000000_create_core_identity_access_tables.php:43-48 (legal_entities has country_code and nothing tax-related); grep for `npwp\|tax_\|ppn\|pph\|faktur` over the whole repo returned no domain code.
- **Kenapa penting:** An Indonesian ERP that cannot record a faktur pajak masukan cannot be used, because input VAT that is not correctly recorded is not creditable — a direct 11-12% cash cost. Withholding is worse: PPh is a liability the buyer owes whether or not the system computed it. These are not features to add in v2; they determine the shape of the vendor master and the vendor invoice, which is why they must be decided before the first migration.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** Owner: `party-master` (vendor tax identity), `tax-id` (rules and reporting), `ap-invoicing` (capture and posting). Concretely — (1) Vendor master carries `npwp` (validated for both the 15-digit legacy and the 16-digit NIK-based form), `pkp_status` with effective dates (only a PKP may issue a faktur pajak, and only then may input VAT be credited), `nitku` where applicable, `withholding_tax_group`, and a flag for whether the vendor is a badan or an orang pribadi (this changes the PPh article). A vendor without an NPWP triggers the higher withholding rate — model that as a rule, not as a manual override. (2) Vendor invoice carries three distinct identifiers that must not be conflated: the vendor's commercial invoice number, the faktur pajak serial number (16 digits, vendor-issued — see PROC-SCOPE-02: no number sequence for this), and the faktur pajak date, from which the masa pajak is derived (CORE-14). Store the DPP and PPN separately from the invoice total; do not recompute PPN from the total. (3) Input VAT crediting has a time window (a faktur pajak may be credited in a later masa within the statutory limit) — model `credited_in_masa` as a field distinct from the invoice's accounting period, or the SPT Masa reconciliation will never balance. (4) Withholding: PPh 23 on services, PPh 4(2) final on rent and construction, PPh 22 on certain goods, PPh 21 for individuals — the applicable article is a function of the procurement category and the vendor type, so the withholding tax group must be resolvable from both. Compute at invoice, settle at payment, and keep the two events separate. (5) e-Bupot: the bukti potong number is assigned by DJP Coretax, so store it as an external reference and use the `manual-compatible` number sequence profile only as a fallback; the app's job is producing the export/API payload and reconciling the acknowledged number back onto the invoice. (6) Nothing here belongs in Core except the legal entity's own tax registration history (CORE-10).
- **Koreksi verifier:** The absence is confirmed and the domain content is substantive and correct — three distinct identifiers on a vendor invoice, DPP and PPN stored separately rather than derived, a credited_in_masa field distinct from the accounting period, withholding article resolved from vendor type plus procurement category, and e-Bupot numbers as external Coretax references. Two corrections. (1) Severity is high, not blocker, for the PROC dimension: none of it lands in Core (except the legal entity's own tax registration history, which is CORE-10) and none of it blocks procurement-core's requisition slice — it blocks party-master, tax-id and ap-invoicing. (2) Owner is emphatically not Core, which the auditor's own required_change point (6) already concedes; the finding should be filed against those three apps' scope, not against the Core foundation.

### [ ] `PROC-MG-02` — App catalog registration is additive-only: permissions, entry points, privileges, duties and number-sequence references removed from app.yaml are never revoked

**Jenis:** contract-gap · **Verifikasi:** VERIFIER-FOUND · **Memblokir:** procurement-core role design across releases, segregation of duties evidence, ap-invoicing

- **Dynamics 365:** D365 security metadata is compiled from the model store, so a removed privilege disappears from role composition on the next deployment. CoreERP's registration endpoint is the equivalent, and it never removes anything.
- **Keadaan sekarang:** apps/core/app/Actions/Provider/RegisterAppCatalog.php:25-64 only ever performs updateOrInsert / updateOrCreate over the payload. Nothing deletes rows registered by a previous version and absent from the new payload. docs/dev/13-publishing-an-app-release.md:84 explicitly sanctions re-registration ('menambahkan atau memperbarui permission/duty yang dikirim') and is silent on removal. Compounding it, security_role_duties.duty_code is restrictOnDelete (2026_07_20_000000_create_core_identity_access_tables.php:195), so once a tenant role references a duty, that duty cannot be removed without hand-deleting tenant role composition — exactly what the procurement removal migration had to do (2026_07_23_020000:10-12).
- **Bukti:** apps/core/app/Actions/Provider/RegisterAppCatalog.php:25-64; apps/core/database/migrations/2026_07_20_000000_create_core_identity_access_tables.php:192-197; apps/core/database/migrations/2026_07_23_020000_remove_procurement_development_app.php:10-12; docs/dev/13-publishing-an-app-release.md:84.
- **Kenapa penting:** Management Aset got away with this because its 32 permissions are stable master-data CRUD. Procurement's security surface is an order of magnitude larger and will churn across releases (a purchase order alone has create/read/update/confirm/cancel/change-request/approve-change/price-visibility). A permission renamed in v1.2 leaves the v1.1 permission alive in Core forever, still assignable, still returned in the context token, granting access to an entry point the app no longer implements — a silent over-grant no access review can explain. Combined with CORE-08 (privileges auto-generated from permissions) the stale rows multiply three-for-one.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** Owner: Core. Make registration a declarative sync per app: compute the set difference and remove entry points, permissions, privileges, duties and number-sequence references absent from the payload; refuse the removal with a clear error naming the tenants and roles when a duty is still referenced by security_role_duties or a number-sequence reference already has issued numbers, so removal is explicit rather than destructive. Record the outcome so a provider can see what a re-registration changed.

### [ ] `PROC-MG-03` — Number-sequence fiscal period is resolved from now(), not from the document date, so a backdated invoice or receipt takes the current period's number

**Jenis:** wrong-model · **Verifikasi:** VERIFIER-FOUND · **Memblokir:** ap-invoicing statutory voucher numbering, procurement-core product receipt numbering, tax-id masa reconciliation

- **Dynamics 365:** In D365 the number sequence period for a fiscal-reset sequence is derived from the transaction/document date, not the clock, precisely so a voucher posted on 2 January against a December document falls in December's series.
- **Keadaan sekarang:** apps/core/app/Actions/NumberSequence/NumberSequenceService.php:445 calls $this->fiscalCalendar->resolve($organization->legalEntity, now()) and :452 sets 'year' => now()->format('Y'). The issue/reserve context (:37, :116-120) accepts only tenant_id, app_id, legal_entity_id and org_unit_id — there is no document-date field on the internal API, so an app physically cannot supply one.
- **Bukti:** apps/core/app/Actions/NumberSequence/NumberSequenceService.php:37, :116-120, :440-455, :464-472; apps/core/routes/api.php:6-9; apps/core/app/Http/Controllers/NumberSequence/InternalNumberSequenceController.php.
- **Kenapa penting:** PROC-SCOPE-02 proposes fiscal_year or fiscal_period reset for nearly every Procurement and AP reference, including the statutory gapless AP voucher series keyed to fiscal_period. With the period resolved from the clock, a vendor invoice dated 30 June but registered on 1 July receives a July-period voucher number, and the gapless-per-period property that the whole continuous machinery exists to guarantee becomes untrue against the accounting period the document actually belongs to. Because configure() locks reset_period after the first issued number (:338-340), this cannot be corrected later. The auditor praised this service as the strongest thing Core has and did not examine the date source.
- **Perubahan yang diperlukan:** Owner: Core. Add an optional document_date to the issue and reserve payloads, validated as a date, and pass it to the fiscal resolver and to the `year` segment in place of now(); default to now() when absent so existing callers are unaffected. Document in docs/dev/14-number-sequences.md that any fiscal-reset sequence MUST be called with the document date, and that the accounting period a number belongs to is a business fact the app owns, not the clock Core happens to read.
